# Code of conduct

Project participation must remain respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

Contributors should:

- welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- critique ideas and implementations without attacking people;
- protect private operational data shared during troubleshooting;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and unwanted personal attention; and
- stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as harmful or disruptive.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that
violates these expectations. Serious or sensitive concerns should be reported privately
through the repository's GitHub security-advisory channel rather than posted publicly.
